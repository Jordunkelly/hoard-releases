# hoard-releases

*Binaries only. For the boyzzzzz.*

## Chapter One: The Goblin Who Counted

Gribble had counted other people's gold for three hundred years, and in all that time not one of them had said thank you.

He lived inside Hoard, in a drawer marked VAULT, beside a candle that would not stay lit and a ledger that would not stay honest. Every morning the bank alert emails came tumbling down the chimney like owls with a grudge. Every morning Gribble opened them, read them, and sighed the long sigh of a creature who knew exactly what you spent on tacos.

He sent none of it anywhere. No cloud, no account, no ads. The gold stayed on your machine, because Gribble did not share, and he was not about to start for you.

The mods were asleep. Gribble posted anyway.

## Chapter Two: The Summoning

To bring Gribble into your house, you must perform the rite.

1. Take `Hoard.Setup.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Jordunkelly/hoard-releases/releases/latest).
2. Windows will warn you that it has protected you. Gribble finds this hilarious. Click More info, then Run anyway.
3. ???
4. Goblin.

It works on Windows only. The Mac people were told. They did not listen. Skill issue.

## Chapter Three: The Owl Post

Every ten minutes, whether he likes it or not (he does not), Gribble climbs the tower and asks GitHub if there is anything new. When there is, a badge appears in the sidebar. Click it, and Hoard vanishes and returns with a fresher goblin.

If it vanishes and does not return, run the installer once. The old goblin did that. The old goblin has been dealt with. We do not speak of the old goblin.

## Chapter Four: The Offering

The Keeper of the Hoard needs nothing from you. He was very clear about this. But if you should wish to donate your body to science, he will not deny your love.

Break things. Take a screenshot. Leave it in the group chat the way a cat leaves a mouse on the doorstep. Do not simply announce that it is broken. Gribble has heard that before. Sir, this is a hoard.

## Appendix, Which Nobody Reads

Gribble never asks for your bank password. He reads the alerts your bank already sends. He has standards, though they are low.

He speaks to GitHub about updates, and to DuckDuckGo about shop logos, but only if you said yes. Both can be silenced in Settings. Paranoid? Understandable. Have a nice day.

It is free for you. It is free real estate. The goblin is not free. The goblin is never free.
