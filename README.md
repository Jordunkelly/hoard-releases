# hoard-releases

Oi. Gribble here.

For three hundred years I guarded a dragon's gold. Then the dragon went broke on subscriptions, and now I guard yours. I count every coin by candlelight. I remember every taco. I forgive nothing.

**This is Hoard.** A budget app that is secretly a goblin RPG. It reads your bank alerts, sorts every coin, and judges you. Keep the fire lit and you climb from Wyrmling to Hoardlord. Let it die and I tell everyone. Everything stays on your PC. No cloud. No ads. No mercy.

**Donate your body to science.** Install it. Break it. Screenshot the crime scene and drop it in the group chat. Testers get a free Steam key at launch. That's the deal. I don't make deals twice.

[Grab the latest build](https://github.com/Jordunkelly/hoard-releases/releases/latest). Windows only. It updates itself. You're welcome.
